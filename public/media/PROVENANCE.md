`../audio/*.mp3` are synthetic neural voice recordings regenerated with edge-tts 7.2.8. The archive narrator uses `en-US-BrianMultilingualNeural` speaking the Polish script; radio/gameplay lines use `pl-PL-ZofiaNeural`. Files are gently mastered with FFmpeg (high-pass 60 Hz, low-pass 7600 Hz, loudness normalization) while the game applies additional archive/radio/spatial processing at runtime. Texts are in `../audio/transcript.json`, regeneration script in `scripts/generate-voices.py`. These are synthetic voices, not recordings or imitations of named actors.

Game material textures, doctor geometry, document animation and noise sounds are generated by project code.
